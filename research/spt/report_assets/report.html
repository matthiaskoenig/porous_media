Reading the figures

Numerical analysis

Whole-lobule means and population standard deviations use cell-volume weights. Spatial profiles use normalized position: periportal 0 to perivenous 1. Empty bins remain missing.

Time and units

Matched statistics interpolate cell values and volumes before reduction, without extrapolation. Native histories retain their own endpoints. Quantitative necrosis plots show percent; geometry legends may show fractions. Source pressure, volume, and flow units remain unspecified where metadata do not define them.

Comparisons

Convergence differences use the finest available mesh as reference. They do not estimate a continuum solution. Dose numbers follow the generator's flow ordering. Consult each figure's provenance for fields, sampling times, ranges, and transforms.
